Entreprise et contexte

  • Leading a team of six Country Medical Affairs Heads, you will play a pivotal role in aligning country medical organizations with International Medical priorities while fostering a high-performing, connected, and future-ready Medical Affairs organization.
  • Reporting to the SERCE Cluster Medical Affairs Head, you will influence medical outcomes across multiple countries, accelerate innovation, strengthen medical capabilities, and help ensure patients gain access to transformative therapies.
  • This is a unique opportunity to combine strategic leadership, people development, and medical excellence while contributing to Novartis' mission to reimagine medicine. Job Description
  • Lead Medical Launch Readiness planning and execution, ensuring organizations are prepared for launches and lifecycle milestones.
  • Oversee evidence generation activities and Integrated Evidence Plans, translating insights into actionable recommendations.
  • Drive excellence in scientific engagement, field medical performance, and Medical Science Liaison deployment across the cluster.
  • Maintain oversight of medical governance, compliance, risk management, audit readiness, and medical standards.
  • Partner with Commercial, Value & Access, Regulatory, Development, and International Medical colleagues to advance strategic priorities.
  • Lead healthcare ecosystem readiness initiatives and support evidence-based approaches that improve patient outcomes and access.
  • Build, coach, and develop a high-performing team of six Country Medical Affairs Heads, fostering collaboration, succession planning, and leadership growth.
