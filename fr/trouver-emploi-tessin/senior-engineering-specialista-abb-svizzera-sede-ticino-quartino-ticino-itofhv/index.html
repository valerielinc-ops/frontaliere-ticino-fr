  • Your role and responsibilities
  • In this role, you will be responsible for Product Engineering, ensuring that technology, assembly methods, and processes follow the ABB guidelines and proper requirements.
  • You will manage the entire product cycle, from the development (working together with R&D) to life cycle management.
  • Furthermore, you will showcase your expertise by defining the correct validation process to achieve the target product robustness.
  • The work model for the role is onsite. #LI-Onsite
  • This role is contributing to the Power Protection business in Quartino.
  • The main stakeholders are Quality, R&D, Production, and After-sales Service. You will be mainly accountable for:
  • Cooperating with R&D to find the best suitable solution for the industrialization of the product.
  • Checking and validating technical deliverables (validation protocols, DFMEA analysis…) during the development of a new product.
  • Checking and validating technical solutions introduced during the life cycle management of the product.
