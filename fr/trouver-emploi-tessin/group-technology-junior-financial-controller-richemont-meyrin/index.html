  • As part of Group Finance and based in Meyrin, your responsibilities will encompass, but are not limited to, the following key areas: Drive Financial Performance & Control Oversee day-to-day financial operations for our technology domains and cost centers, ensuring efficient and appropriate financial control systems.
  • Actively contribute to monthly, quarterly, and annual financial closing activities, including preparing accruals, reviewing commitments, and assisting with allocation cycles.
  • Partner closely with Group Technology Managers to foster cost awareness and enhance understanding of financial performance, providing insightful analysis and ad-hoc reports.
  • Support the tracking and monitoring of resources for key technology projects and initiatives.
  • Master Planning & Forecasting Play a key role in the planning and forecasting cycles (Budget, Latest Estimate, Roadmap), from data collection and analysis to presentation and report preparation.
  • Assist in the review and consolidation of budget components (Investments, Operating expenses, Headcounts) for supported areas.
  • Prepare preliminary management reports and conduct initial variance analysis against financial targets.
  • Ensure Financial Integrity & Compliance Support the Capital Approval Request (CAR) process and contribute to the follow-up of financial commitments, including budget assumption validation and vendor contract checks.
