Description originale

About Join the House of Fusalp

Founded in Annecy in 1952 by two master tailors, Fusalp has spent over 70 years redefining skiwear by combining technical innovation, exceptional craftsmanship and timeless elegance.

Today, Fusalp brings The Art of the Mountain to life across the world's most iconic mountain destinations and international cities. Our collections move seamlessly from the slopes to the city, guided by one conviction: performance and elegance should never be separated.

This role supports our Gstaad boutique during the winter season. We are looking for a client-facing profile who wants to build a career in premium retail and grow through strong service and commercial performance.

Job Description

  • Create a warm, personalised and memorable experience for every client.
  • Understand client needs and recommend products, silhouettes and complete looks with confidence.
  • Contribute actively to sales targets, KPIs, clienteling and repeat business.
  • Share Fusalp's heritage, craftsmanship and technical expertise.
  • Maintain impeccable visual merchandising and boutique presentation.
  • Take part in all daily operations, including stock, deliveries, replenishment, inventories and cash handling

Preferred Experience What will help you succeed

For this role, potential and attitude matter, but we still expect a credible first experience facing customers.

Gstaad combines an international clientele with high service expectations. You will spend most of your time facing clients, but everyone also supports stock, deliveries and boutique operations.

Must-have

  • A first successful customer-facing experience, ideally in retail, fashion, premium hospitality or another high-quality service environment.
  • English is required
  • A genuine desire to build a career in retail rather than simply take a seasonal job.
  • Enthusiasm for selling, learning and working towards commercial goals.
  • Professionalism, reliability and the willingness to contribute wherever the boutique needs you.
  • Availability to work a flexible rota, including weekends, public holidays and peak periods.

Nice-to-have

  • German, French or any additional language.
  • Previous experience in premium or luxury retail.
  • Interest in fashion, ski or mountain lifestyle.

What you’ll love working here

  • Seasonal 100% contract from December 2026 to the end of March 2027.
  • CHF 4,500 per month + bonus. The salary is flexible and may be adjusted based on the candidate’s profile, qualifications, and experience.
  • Continuous training in products, client experience and retail excellence.
  • The opportunity to gain strong experience in one of Fusalp's key Alpine destinations.

Recruitment Process If your profile matches the role, you will first meet our HR team before continuing the conversation with the boutique and retail management teams
