Responsabilités principales

  • Design and plan geotechnical engineering solutions in soil and rock for complex projects with some or minimum support.
  • Participate in geotechnical investigation campaigns, interpreting tests and monitoring technical data for decision-making.
  • Prepare technical reports, define deliverables and ensure compliance with schedules and quality standards.
  • Carry out technical site visits, collaborate with customers, suppliers and internal teams, and propose innovative and efficient solutions for each challenge.
  • Capability of collaborate with cross-discipline project teams in the delivery of special packages for complex projects.

Exigences principales

  • Degree in Civil Engineering and specialization in geotechnics.
  • Minimum experience of 7 years in geotechnical design, preferably in engineering, consulting, linked to large-scale projects.
  • First experience in the preparation of technical-economic proposals.
  • Good knowledge in design and geotechnical and structural analysis applied to tunnels, slopes, foundations, containment structures, dams and underground works.
  • Analytical, organized and results-oriented profile, with good communication skills and ability to work in a team and solve problems.
  • Good proficiency in the use of technical software such as Rockscience and PLAXIS/FLAC.
  • Proficiency in English; knowledge of Italian.
