Vue d’ensemble du poste

Timberland EMEA is looking for an exceptional Associate Merchandiser, Footwear – DTC to join our Timberland EMEA team based in Stabio, Switzerland.

Founded in 1973, Timberland® is a global outdoor lifestyle brand.

Best known for its original yellow boot designed for the harsh elements of New England, Timberland today offers a full range of footwear, apparel and accessories for people who value purposeful style and share the brand’s passion for enjoying - and protecting - nature.
