Director, PAMP.com — MKS PAMP SA, Genève (TI).

MISSION The MKS PAMP Group intends to establish PAMP.com as a direct-to-consumer channel across multiple global markets.

The role has ownership of the PAMP.com DTC channel from platform to customer, across both website and app, excluding B2B and institutional channels.
