  • Your Responsibilities

Responsabilités principales

  • Your Responsibilities
  • Conduct direct and collaborative engagements with corporate issuers on strategic ESG issues
  • Take ownership of developing and driving thematic engagement topics (e.g. strategic minerals, human rights)
  • Participate in proxy voting activities by identifying and analysing critical general meetings in line with our voting policy
  • Support the operational aspects of proxy voting activities
  • Maintain robust engagement records and contribute actively to RfPs, proxy voting reports for clients and annual reviews
  • Contribute to high-quality ESG and Stewardship-related research and conduct sectoral ESG analysis

Exigences principales

  • University degree in business, economics, finance, or another field relevant for sustainable finance, with a minimum of 5 years of practical experience in asset management and/or sustainable finance and first relevant work experience in financial services
  • Interest in non-financial risk and sustainability topics; basic knowledge in the area of stewardship is an advantage
  • Excellent Microsoft Office skills; knowledge in data management (SQL) and/or Python are additional assets
  • Strong analytical and problem solving skills with the ability to weigh problems and set priorities
