Entreprise et contexte

  • Support the exploration and sourcing of fabrics, trims, organization/maintenance of archive & PLM as well as design details relevant to assigned product categories.
  • Maintain and update design documentation, sketches, color-ups, seasonal product information and product configuration system in FLEX and DASH throughout the development process.
  • Collaborate closely with Designers, Material Specialist, cross-functional partners and headquarters in Denver to support day-to-day product creation activities. Skills that will make you succeed
  • A degree in Fashion Design or Textile Engineering is required given the nature of the role.
  • Strong understanding of the materials, design process, including outdoor, lifestyle and technical products.
  • Proficient in Excel, Adobe Creative Suite, with strong Illustrator skills.
  • Creative, proactive, and open-minded, with the ability to take initiative.
  • Strong teamwork and collaboration skills, working effectively within cross functional teams.
  • Highly organized, adaptable, and able to meet deadlines in fast-paced environments.
  • A keen interest in design, materials innovation, sustainability, outdoor and performance products, and emerging consumer trends.
