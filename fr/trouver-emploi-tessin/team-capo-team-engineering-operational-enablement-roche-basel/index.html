Responsabilités principales

  • Job responsibilities
  • Strategic & Team Leadership: Build and lead a high-performing team of data modelers and support architecture specialists.
  • Establish the department's operational roadmap, service intake model, and capacity planning.
  • Support Model & CMDB Governance: Act as the accountable authority for CMDB data quality standards, data modeling guidelines, and structural support frameworks across all product teams.
  • Cross-Functional Consulting: Partner with Product Managers and Product Teams to translate complex business and technical contexts into robust operational support structures, eliminating the burden of low-frequency, high-complexity modeling on individual teams.
  • Work with Observability and SRE teams for Standardizing our knowledge and its relationships unifies disparate tools (Grafana, Splunk, etc.).
  • Quality Assurance & Compliance: Define performance metrics, continuous monitoring, health checks, periodic reviews, enforcement mechanisms, and automated validation rules to maintain 100% accurate dependencies, impact maps, and ticket routing logic.
  • Process Enablement & Frameworks: Standardize playbooks, intake frameworks, and self-service guidelines to streamline how product teams interface with IT Service Management systems.
  • Contextual layer for operational telemetry (dependencies, ownership, business mapping) to enable reasoning.
  • AI Tools and Automation Adoption: Define and adopt AI-driven Solutions and rulesets to support the identification of operational support structure gaps and the automation of the corrective actions in the CMDB.

Processus de candidature

  • You will bridge the gap between business application contexts and complex data structures, leading a team of experts dedicated to ensuring high-quality IT service management data, seamless automated ticket routing, and accurate impact analysis across all enterprise products.
  • You will collaborate directly with Product Managers to translate complex business contexts into accurate CMDB dependency trees, automated ticket routing rules, and impact analysis structures.
  • You will be responsible for ensuring our 300+ enterprise products maintain flawless operational support models without burdening software development teams.
