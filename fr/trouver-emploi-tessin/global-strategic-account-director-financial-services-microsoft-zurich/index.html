Vue d’ensemble du poste

Overview Microsoft's Financial Services organization is seeking an accomplished Global Strategic Account Director to lead one of our most important and strategic global client relationships. This role represents one of Microsoft's most consequential leadership opportunities within the Financial Services industry. The Global Strategic Account Director serves as Microsoft's lead business executive, accountable for the overall executive relationship, strategic partnership, and long-term business outcomes across the account. The successful candidate will combine deep industry expertise, executive credibility, transformational leadership, and technical acumen to help the customer accelerate its AI-powered business transformation while strengthening Microsoft's position as a trusted strategic partner. This role requires the ability to engage and influence at the Board, Group Executive Board, and C-suite level, shape transformation agendas, mobilize One Microsoft, and create measurable business impact through innovation, technology, and execution. Microsoft’s mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. As employees we come together with a growth mindset, innovate to empower others, and collaborate to realize our shared goals. Each day we build on our values of respect, integrity, and accountability to create a culture of inclusion where everyone can thrive at work and beyond.
