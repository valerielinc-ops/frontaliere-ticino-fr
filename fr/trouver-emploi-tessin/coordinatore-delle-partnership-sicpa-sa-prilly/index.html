Vue d’ensemble du poste

Reporting to the Partnerships Director, the Partnerships Coordinator will provide day-to-day operational support in managing and optimizing the Group's interests across private joint venture (JV) companies.

These JVs are private-to-private alliances formed specifically to develop and capture Business-to-Government (B2G) market opportunities.

The Partnerships Coordinator will act as the operational anchor, ensuring smooth collaboration between SICPA and its private local partners as they jointly deploy SICPA solutions to government clients. ROLE JV and Partnership Governance & Compliance
