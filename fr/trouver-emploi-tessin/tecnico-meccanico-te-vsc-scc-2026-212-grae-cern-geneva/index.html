Vue d’ensemble du poste

Are you looking for an opportunity to contribute to the Future Circular Collider (FCC), one of the most ambitious scientific projects ever envisioned?

Help develop the coating technology that will equip its vacuum chambers with a Non-Evaporable Getter thin film.

Working alongside experienced engineers, technicians and physicists, you will contribute to the design, fabrication and coating.

  • Are you looking for an opportunity to contribute to the Future Circular Collider (FCC), one of the most ambitious scientific projects ever envisioned?
  • Help develop the coating technology that will equip its vacuum chambers with a Non-Evaporable Getter thin film.
  • Your responsibilities:
  • Design & construction of mechanical parts for customised physical vapor deposition systems and associated tooling.
  • Experience in machining metal parts in lathe and milling machines.
  • CNC would be an advantage.

Responsabilités principales

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Design & construction of mechanical parts for customised physical vapor deposition systems and associated tooling.
  • Assembly of coating setups operating in Ultra High Vacuum.
  • Validation of the coating technology and process (in real scale prototypes up to 12 meter long beam pipes).
  • Validation of the vacuum performance of the coatings produced (in Ultra High Vacuum systems).

  • CNC would be an advantage.

  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
