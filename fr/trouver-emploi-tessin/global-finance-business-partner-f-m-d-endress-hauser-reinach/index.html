Responsabilités principales

  • Working closely with the Global Finance Business Partner, you will lead Group-level activities while ensuring effective cross-coverage for selected Product Center responsibilities.
  • Which tasks will you perform? You will:
  • Lead Group-wide planning and forecasting activities, including the consolidation and reporting of rolling forecasts for Operations Reviews, year-end meetings, Executive Board and Supervisory Board reporting.
  • You will coordinate timelines, instructions and submission quality
  • Deliver high-quality Group management reporting, including the monthly Executive Board reporting package, facts and figures, quick forecasts, annual Cost of Sales reporting, and selected reporting obligations to government bodies and trade associations
  • Drive consistent Cost of Sales and standard costing practices by acting as the department’s subject-matter expert, partnering with Production and local controllers on methodology, costing questions, variance analysis, and Group-wide harmonization
  • Provide actionable performance insights through e.g. margin analysis, profit elimination calculations, working capital reviews, currency exposure analysis etc.
  • You will act as a first point of contact for Production finance teams on FP&A, planning, costing, and performance-related questions
  • Shape the transformation of Group controlling by leading the implementation of the Group Management Accounting tool and promoting harmonized management accounting and Cost of Sales practices across the Group
  • Coordinate key Group finance activities, including incentive calculations, year-end transfer pricing topics, and contributions to the Corporate Risk Map process.
