Vue d’ensemble du poste

Join the ATLAS Data Acquisition (DAQ) team and contribute to the modernisation of one of the world's most advanced scientific computing networks.

Working alongside experienced network engineers, you will gain hands-on experience in the deployment, testing, operation, and monitoring of a high-availability, multi-terabit-per-second network that underpins the ATLAS experiment, while helping to develop future-proof monitoring and alerting solutions in collaboration with experts across CERN and the LHC experiments. of CERN

Responsabilités principales

  • Your responsibilities
  • Participate in the installation, commissioning, testing, and operation of network infrastructure supporting the ATLAS Data Acquisition (DAQ) system.
  • Deploy and maintain a production-ready network monitoring and alerting solution for critical DAQ services and equipment.
  • Collaborate with experts from other LHC experiments to identify common requirements and contribute to the development of scalable, future-proof monitoring solutions.
  • Support the operation and upkeep of the ATLAS TDAQ laboratory, including infrastructure, equipment, and resource management activities.
  • Contribute to the maintenance and evolution of the ATLAS DAQ hardware infrastructure, including server deployment, hardware interventions, and related technical support.

Exigences principales

  • Basic experience as Sysadmin or Netadmin.
  • Hands-on experience with server and/or switch installations in professional environment. Skills
  • Basic knowledge of TCP/IP stack and Ethernet networks.
  • Basic programming skills in Python and Bash.
  • General knowledge of Linux systems.
  • Ability to work in teams.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French. Eligibility criteria:
  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
