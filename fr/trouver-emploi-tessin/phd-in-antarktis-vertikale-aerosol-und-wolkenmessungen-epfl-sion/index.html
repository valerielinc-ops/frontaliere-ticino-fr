Description originale

Mission

The Extreme Environments Research Laboratory (EERL) is looking for a PhD student for our Antarctica InSync activities. Within Antarctica InSync we will study aerosol-cloud interactions over coastal Antarctica and the Southern Ocean sea ice with instruments placed on a tethered balloon and at ground.

Your mission will be to perform and analyze vertically resolved measurements of aerosols, trace gases and meteorological variables using EERL’s helikite facility and to conduct ground based aerosol chemical observations using the new nano-mechanical membrane infrared spectroscopy method (NEMS-FTIR) that we developed.

The EERL focuses on understanding natural and anthropogenic atmospheric processes, as well as exchange processes between the atmosphere and cryosphere, ocean and land that have climate impact and are impacted by climate. Within Antarctica InSync, the EERL will deploy instruments on the icebreaker Polarstern that will drift in the Weddell Sea and at Neumayer station as part of the DOMINO project.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Perform field observations with a small team in Antarctica using the helikite facility
  • Enhance the helikite's measurement capabilities by adding new instrumentation for chemical analyses and cloud observations with the support of a technician
  • Analyze samples of aerosols and trace gases in laboratory, for e.g., chemical composition and ice nucleating particles
  • Data analysis of observations, numerical model output and remote sensing data
  • Tight collaboration with the project partners and participation in working meetings
  • Teaching assistantship
  • Part-time supervision of MSc students

Profile

  • A master degree or (equivalent) in atmospheric, climate, or environment science, engineering, meteorology, physics or similar
  • Interest in atmospheric studies and polar regions
  • Desire to do field work
  • Desire to do laboratory work
  • Interested in improving the tethered balloon payload with support of EERL's technician
  • Capable of conducting scientific research independently
  • Basic knowledge of scientific programming
  • Data handling skills
  • Good interpersonal skills, team player
  • Good knowlege of English (written and spoken)

We offer

  • A dynamic, interdisciplinary and young research group
  • A vibrant, internationally visible and rising academic institution in alpine and polar environments research
  • A great working atmosphere and ample opportunities for professional and personal development with access to many continuing education courses
  • 4 years to complete your PhD with a competitive remuneration
  • A 1-year fixed-term contract (CDD), renewable for 4 years
