Entreprise et contexte

  • The Global Data, Digital & AI (GDDA) organization plays a central role in Roche Pharma Commercial’s digital and AI ecosystem.
  • GDDA sets the enterprise digital strategy, translates commercial objectives into a coordinated portfolio of investments, and defines the standards and operating model that enable scalable execution across programs and products.
  • By connecting strategy, portfolio, standards, programs, and products, GDDA ensures that data, digital, and AI capabilities are delivered as scalable, enterprise solutions that drive adoption and improve outcomes for patients, healthcare providers, and the broader healthcare ecosystem. The Opportunity
  • The Head of Digital Strategy, Excellence & Standards is responsible for shaping and driving the strategic agenda of the Global Data, Digital & AI organization within Pharma Commercial.
  • This role leads development of the GDDA strategic plan, ensuring strategic coherence with GDDA’s core mandates while translating enterprise and Pharma Commercial priorities into clear GDDA objectives, outcomes, and prioritization principles that guide investment decisions.
  • This role also drives the design of GDDA’s operating model and organizational approach including the definition of global vs. local standards and operating discipline for digital products, data, and technology— establishing the bar for quality and consistency across markets and local execution.
  • This role also owns change enablement across the digital ecosystem — ensuring that new standards, processes, and capabilities are adopted effectively through structured communication, stakeholder engagement, and organizational readiness.
  • Partnering closely with leaders across Roche Pharma, the Head of Strategy defines interfaces, aligns initiatives, and avoids duplication across the broader digital ecosystem.
  • Acting as Chief of Staff to the GDDA Leader, this role represents GDDA perspectives in leadership forums, develops executive narratives, and ensures strong cross-functional alignment and a coherent path from strategy to impact.
