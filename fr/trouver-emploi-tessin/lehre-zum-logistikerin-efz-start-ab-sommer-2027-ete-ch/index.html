Vue d’ensemble du poste

Are you an organizational talent, reliable and persistent?

Do you enjoy technical devices, have technical skills and have a high sense of responsibility?

If this is true and you have good grades from secondary or secondary school, training to become a logistics specialist might be right for you!

As a logistics specialist EFZ, a 3-year apprenticeship awaits you.

With us you can work in various warehouse areas and get to know all facets of warehouse logistics work.

At the beginning you will be introduced by experienced logisticians and you will carry out various, more manual tasks.

Ceux-ci sont possibles

  • Tasks primarily related to the picking and provision of smaller goods, promotional items and the like.

Dès que vous avez suivi le cours d'utilisation des transpalettes électriques, vous êtes autorisé à effectuer le premier déplacement mécanique interne des marchandises.

In addition to the lessons at the vocational school in Olten, you will learn in practice the correct handling of receiving and issuing goods.

If you have successfully completed the inter-company forklift course, you will be able to store and unload palletized goods independently.
