Responsabilités principales

  • Your role and responsibilities
  • In this role, you will have the opportunity to provide accounting, reporting, and consolidation expertise.
  • Each day, you will support in accounting and reporting matters to business/reporting units through resolution of accounting and reporting questions, financial statement preparation and review follow-up on accounting issues.
  • You will also showcase your expertise by supporting in the preparation of financial reports in compliance with internal guidelines, procedures, and external regulatory requirements.
  • You will be the business process owner of the local R2R process and the main speaking partner for internal and external auditors. The work model for the role is: hybrid
  • This role is contributing to the MO Finance CoE and Front Office / Motion Finance in Baden, Switzerland.
  • Main stakeholders are Company Controllers, Group Reporting and financial Managers. You will be mainly accountable for:
  • Manage and ensure the accuracy of general accounting activities, including pension and lease accounting, supporting reliable financial reporting and transparency.
  • Act as the local Record-to-Report (R2R) Business Process Owner, driving effective internal controls, supporting testing activities, and fostering cross-functional collaboration across diverse teams.
  • Ensure compliance with US GAAP, Swiss GAAP, and ABB policies, performing quality reviews, analyzing financial data, and partnering with internal and external auditors to maintain high reporting standards.

Exigences principales

  • for the role
  • Proven experience in Finance and Accounting, with a strong background in General Accounting, Financial Reporting and/or process improvement initiatives.
  • Strong knowledge of internal controls and governance requirements, ideally within the Record-to-Report (R2R) process.
  • Solid experience working with SAP FI/CO; knowledge of Hyperion and SAP BW is an advantage.
  • Advanced Excel skills and a data-driven approach to financial analysis and reporting.
  • Sound understanding of Swiss GAAP and US GAAP accounting standards.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a structured, accurate and quality-focused way of working.
  • Degree in Economics, Finance, Business Administration or a related field, and/or a recognized professional qualification in Finance and Accounting.
  • Fluency in German and English, with the ability to communicate effectively and collaborate with diverse stakeholders in an international environment.
