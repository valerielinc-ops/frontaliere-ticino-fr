Description originale

The Bühler Group is a Swiss, family-owned technology company specializing in food, feed and advanced materials. Every day, around 2 billion people consume food processed with Bühler technologies, while mobility solutions using Bühler technology serve 1 billion people.

This global impact comes with responsibility. Bühler aims to reduce energy, waste and water use by 50% in its customers' value chains. To measure progress, the company has quantified the environmental footprint of key products and assessed how its technologies and services can reduce CO₂ emissions, land use and water consumption.

Bühler offers a six-month internship to further embed this methodology across the business, support customer projects and reduce environmental impacts in both customer and Bühler value chains. You will gain expertise in carbon accounting, value-chain thinking and entrepreneurship. Shape a sustainable future with us. Apply now! Aufgaben: • Drive the environmental quantification project and further embed the methodology across Bühler's businesses. • Contribute to reducing CO₂e emissions in both the Customer Value Chain and the Bühler Value Chain. • Build and expand your expertise in carbon accounting and value-chain thinking. • Develop your entrepreneurial skills within an international company. • Take ownership of the project and collaborate with relevant stakeholders to overcome challenges and deliver meaningful results. Anforderungen: • You are adaptable and comfortable working on a project that requires both interpersonal and technical skills. • You are a quick learner with strong communication and social skills. • You think entrepreneurially and are able to take ownership of the project, identify opportunities, and overcome challenges. • You are trustworthy and demonstrate ownership in line with Bühler's values: Trust, Ownership, and Passion. • You are passionate about Bühler and motivated by the opportunity to advance sustainability across the business. • You have a good command of written and spoken English; good German language skills are an advantage.
