Responsabilités principales

  • Own and drive the final, highly complex contract negotiations and signature process with one of the top transit partners in France, aiming for a successful contract closing.
  • Coordinate post-launch activities cross-functionally with Marketing and PR teams to define and execute the launch communication plan, ensuring a seamless rollout.

  • Partner with the rest of the BD team to manage key Google Pay Online API accounts (focusing on flagship Transit partners) and execute commercial campaigns to accelerate adoption and transaction volume.

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.

  • Experience in signing and negotiating agreements, business development, partner management, or product management in an internet or technology environment.
  • Experience working with C-level executives and cross-functionally across all levels of management.
  • Ability to communicate in English and French fluently as this is a customer-facing role that requires interactions with local and international stakeholders.
