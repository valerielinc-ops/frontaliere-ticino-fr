Responsabilités principales

  • Your responsibilities As an Agile Coach, you will join the Delivery section within the Business Computing group.
  • Your mission will be to coach, mentor, and train teams and business partners on lean-agile practices in software development, fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, and continuous improvement.
  • We are looking for someone highly motivated and passionate about business agility.
  • The group is currently in the preparation phase for two business transformation projects: one covering Finance, Procurement, and Logistics processes (new ERP system) and, in parallel, the implementation of a new Human Capital Management system.
  • This requires strong coordination and cross-functional collaboration across the group.
  • As an Agile Coach, you will be assigned to one of the projects to contribute to the successful implementation.
  • Being part of the Lean/Agile chapter, you will drive BC Group events, help grow the Agile maturity of all group internal actors (teams, designers, product managers, the group management team itself) and work with the external group stakeholders to increase their awareness, acceptance and embrace of the group's Agile ways of working.
  • You will be aiming to deepen business agility fundamentals and lean portfolio management concepts within the group, leading to a healthier relationship between the group and its business partners. Your responsibilities
