Vue d’ensemble du poste

Your Responsibilities

  • You lead the development of Exhaust Aftertreatment Systems (EATS) from requirements and system design through testing, validation and PPAP
  • You define technical specifications and requirements for EATS components and ensure their alignment with project targets
  • You plan and coordinate development and validation activities, including test plans, prototype and calibration maturity, measurements and resources
  • You coordinate EATS testing on engine test benches, analyses results and drive troubleshooting and problem-solving activities
  • You manage DVPnR (Design Verification Plan and Reporting) activities and support Quality Gates, ensuring validation status, risks and deliverables are properly monitored and documented
  • You work closely with Engine Development, Design, Purchasing and Quality to develop and integrate EATS solutions
  • You act as the technical reference for EATS suppliers, from technology evaluation and component development to technical troubleshooting and 8D activities
  • You contribute to the definition and maintenance of EATS-related software and technical documentation, and report development status to the project team Your Profile
  • You have 3–5 years of experience in EATS, exhaust system or hardware development, ideally in the field of internal combustion engines
  • You have solid knowledge of exhaust aftertreatment systems and catalyst technologies; experience with DEF dosing systems is an advantage
  • You have experience in EATS testing, validation and calibration, including test planning, measurement and data analysis
  • You have knowledge of EATS simulation (1D/3D, with basic CFD knowledge), FMEA and DVPnR methodology
  • You are familiar with measurement and data acquisition tools such as CSM, Vector or ETAS and have a basic understanding of automotive software
  • You have a structured and analytical approach to troubleshooting, enjoy working cross-functionally and are comfortable coordinating technical topics with suppliers Our Offer Can we inspire you to take on a responsible position in a dynamic and independent family business?
