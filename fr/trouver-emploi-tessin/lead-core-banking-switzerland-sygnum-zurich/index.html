Vue d’ensemble du poste

We are looking for a senior Product Manager / Product Owner to lead Sygnum’s Core Banking Switzerland capabilities end-to-end.

The role requires someone who can manage architectural and organisational complexity across the tri-party setup with Sygnum, Swisscom, and Finnova, while focusing the team’s efforts on the work that creates the biggest impact for the bank.

Responsabilités principales

  • Provide subject matter expertise on core banking processes, integrations, and architectural dependencies

  • Support budget planning and financial monitoring for Swisscom and Finnova-related services, changes, and dependencies
