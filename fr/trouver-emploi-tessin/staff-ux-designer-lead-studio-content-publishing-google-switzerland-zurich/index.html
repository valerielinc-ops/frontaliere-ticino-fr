Responsabilités principales

  • Modernize core upload and publish flows, smart drafts, and comprehensive metadata editing to take the anxiety and friction out of publishing.
  • Lead the design of intuitive interfaces that ensure creators can confidently and efficiently publish their content.
  • Craft intelligent, Artificial Intelligence (AI)-powered tools and optimizations for titles, thumbnails, tags, and metadata that maximize a creator’s reach and audience engagement.
  • Transform complex machine learning models into accessible, actionable insights for creators.
  • Empower storytelling through advanced capabilities like title and thumbnail A/B testing and seamless integrations with powerful video editing platforms.
  • Streamline creator workflows from creation through post-production.

Exigences principales

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 8 years of experience in product design or User Experience (UX).
  • Experience designing creator tools, video editing workflows, content management systems (CMS), or media publishing platforms.
  • Experience leading and mentoring cross-functional UX designers and managing multi-year product design goals.
  • Include a portfolio, website, or any other related link to your work in your resume (providing a viewable link or access instructions).
