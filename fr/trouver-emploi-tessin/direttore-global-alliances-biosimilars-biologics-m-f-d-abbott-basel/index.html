Entreprise et contexte

  • Serve as the primary relationship manager and executive point of contact for strategic biologics/biosimilars, ensuring effective communication, alignment, and collaboration across all relevant functions within EPD and partner organizations.
  • Build, maintain, and expand strategic alliances with external partners to maximize the value of existing licensing agreements and support long-term portfolio expansion across global EPD markets.
  • Establish robust governance frameworks with partners, including regular business reviews, joint steering committees, and performance monitoring to ensure successful execution of partnership objectives.
  • Lead the management of partner relationships, proactively identifying opportunities, mitigating risks, and resolving issues to ensure sustainable and mutually beneficial collaborations. Business Development & Licensing
  • Provide leadership in alliance management, business development, and partnership strategy to support the growth objectives of the EPD Global Biologics portfolio.
  • Support the execution of the Global Biologics strategy by identifying, evaluating, and engaging high-quality developers and partners, and by negotiating and executing licensing agreements in collaboration with License & Acquisitions (L&A) and other key stakeholders.
  • Identify unmet business needs and portfolio gaps that can be addressed through strategic partnerships, licensing opportunities, acquisitions, or other external collaborations.
  • Lead and support partner evaluations, due diligence activities, business case development, and internal approval processes for licensing-in opportunities.
  • Identify and execute new business development opportunities by coordinating cross-functional requirements, business case submissions, governance approvals, and contract negotiations. Portfolio Growth & Commercial Execution
  • Drive the expansion of the Global Biologics portfolio by securing partnerships that strengthen the pipeline and support EPD's long-term Biologics growth strategy.
