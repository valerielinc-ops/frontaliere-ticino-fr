Vue d’ensemble du poste

Mission

EPFL is strengthening its capacity to conduct world-leading research involving human biological samples, clinical collaborations, sensitive data, artificial intelligence, and biomedical innovation.

To support this ambition, EPFL is establishing an institutional framework for the management of human biological samples and sensitive research data.

  • Main duties and responsibilities Strategic Development:

Responsabilités principales

  • Main duties and responsibilities Strategic Development:
  • Lead the implementation of EPFL's institutional strategy for human biological samples and sensitive research data.
  • Develop a long-term roadmap for human research infrastructure at EPFL in coordination with the operational teams.
  • Identify opportunities for national and international partnerships and participation in research infrastructures. Governance, Policy, and Compliance:
  • Establish and maintain governance frameworks for human biological samples and sensitive data, including policies for acquisition, access, sharing, reuse, and stewardship.
  • Support institutional alignment with ethical, legal, and regulatory requirements (Swiss HRA, GDPR, data protection, biobanking standards).
  • Promote harmonization and best practices across EPFL laboratories and research units. Cross-Institutional Coordination:
  • Serve as the institutional point of coordination for compliance topics involving human research.
  • Facilitate interactions between researchers, IT services, Legal Affairs, Ethics Affairs, the Data Protection Office, and external partners.
  • Coordinate EPFL’s participation in national and international infrastructures (e.g., biobanking networks, secure data environments, precision medicine initiatives).
