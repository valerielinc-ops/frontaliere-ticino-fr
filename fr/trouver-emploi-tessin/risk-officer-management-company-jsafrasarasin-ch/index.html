Risk Officer Management Company Basel, CH Full-time Unlimited Your Role

As Risk Officer Management Company at J.

Safra Sarasin Investmentfonds AG you are responsible for the management-company risk framework – internal control system, operational risk and oversight – and act as the independent second line of defense across the ManCo’s activities and provide strong process and data-management skills to define and further improve controls, automate controls and reporting, and raise the quality and efficiency of how the function operates.

Responsabilités principales

  • Your Responsibilities
  • Maintain and further develop JSSIF’s Internal Control System; act as a second-line control function ensuring first-line controls are performed, evidenced and remediated
  • Manage operational risk end-to-end: incident detection and follow-up, operational KRI monitoring, key-control-matrix oversight, reporting and mitigation
  • Run the annual Risk & Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) in line with JSS Group Risk requirements
  • Produce structured ManCo risk reporting to the Executive Committee, Risk Committee and Board, and monthly KRI reporting to Group Risk Management
  • Maintain Business Continuity Management and the Business Continuity Plan, including trainings and periodic checks
  • Act as a deputy for the Fund Risk Oversight function and co-own PRIIP KID coordination and maintenance

Exigences principales

  • Higher education in Finance, Business or a related field
  • 4-7 years of practical experience in the field of risk management, internal control or operational risk within a Swiss or Luxembourg management company, fund administration, audit or asset management
  • Working knowledge of the relevant Swiss regulatory environment (FINMA, CISA/CollIA, FinSA/FinIA, AMAS self-regulation); awareness of UCITS/AIFMD and CSSF requirements is an advantage given the Group’s Luxembourg footprint Required
  • Independent, structured and hands-on, comfortable challenging first-line assumptions.
  • Experience in design and automate controls, KRI calculations and reporting, improving data quality, traceability and process efficiency
  • Strong work ethics, integrity, high level of accountability and willingness to take responsibility Fluency in English, German is an advantage Your Benefits
