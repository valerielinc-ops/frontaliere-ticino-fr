About the role Sulzer’s Leeds facility is a global centre of excellence for advanced design and manufacturing, specialising in OEM pump technology and engineered solutions for critical industrial applications. This site brings together cutting-edge engineering capability, digital design, and high-precision manufacturing, supporting sectors such as energy, water, chemicals, and infrastructure. With significant recent investment, our Leeds operations are evolving rapidly—enhancing our capabilities in advanced machining, digital engineering, testing, and product innovation. This is where new business pumps are brought to life from cradle to grave—from initial concept and design through to manufacture, testing, delivery, and lifecycle support. We are seeking a Senior Project Manager to lead the successful delivery of complex, high-value pump projects within our new business portfolio.
