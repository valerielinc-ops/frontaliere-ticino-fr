Description complète

This role makes a significant contribution to ensuring optimal performance in fraud prevention and in authentication and authorisation processes. This is achieved by maximising the authorisation rate of legitimate transactions while, at the same time, promptly identifying and blocking fraudulent transactions.

Responsibilities

  • Collaborates on optimising performance in Fraud Prevention & Authorisation
  • Collaborates on defining and structuring the processes applied in Fraud & Authorisation Services
  • Is responsible for the continuous development and refinement of the prevention and authorisation system
  • Responsible for implementing and maintaining fraud detection rules to minimise false positives
  • Ensures high standards of quality and compliance in authorisation and risk management processes
  • Promptly identifies and analyses emerging digital threats and new fraud trends in order to develop effective mitigation measures
  • Analysis of credit card authorisation data (Oracle Developer, Python, SPSS) in order to develop fraud prevention measures and rules to identify and decline fraudulent transactions.
  • Monitoring the performance of fraud prevention systems and identifying potential areas for improvement.
  • Participation in projects and campaigns to optimise fraud prevention processes, measures and systems.
  • Contribution to developing and creating meaningful reports to measure the performance of systems and processes.
  • Training of employees in specialist know-how.
  • Cross-functional collaboration with other departments (e.g. Customer Service, Risk Management) in handling fraud cases
  • Documentation of processes, rules and operational guidelines, Reports for Management

What do we offer?

  • Flexibility in working from home and in managing working hours, to ensure a healthy work-life balance
  • Numerous benefits and incentives
  • Wellbeing, motivational and team-building activities

For the open position, we are looking for candidates who meet the following requirements:

  • Degree in statistics, mathematics, computer science or related disciplines
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in data analysis
  • Good knowledge of Python and SQL (possibly SPSS, SAS or similar tools)
  • Excellent command of Italian and English (knowledge of German is an advantage)
  • Previous experience in fraud prevention or risk management
  • Experience with data visualisation tools (e.g. Power BI)
  • Familiarity with PSD2 regulations, EMV 3DS2 flows and the most advanced authentication standards, including biometrics and Passkeys
  • Above-average commitment to managing credit card fraud risks
  • Willingness to continuously acquire and develop in-depth specialist knowledge of the credit card sector, with a particular focus on authorisation and fraud
  • Willingness to work independently
  • Initiative and commitment to continuous improvement in one's own area of work
  • Ability to work in a team and willingness to share knowledge
  • Service orientation
  • Strong analytical skills and data orientation
  • Previous experience in a similar role in the financial sector is preferred
  • Stimulating work environment with plenty of room for manoeuvre and autonomy
  • Active support from department management
  • Opportunities for development and professional growth within the position
  • Residence in Ticino or willingness to relocate
