Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

As a 6-month intern in our Hospitality department, you will join a dynamic team and work on a wide range of tasks and projects.

In this role, you will

  • Coordinate the organization of on site events
  • Support the development of animations and participate in the social life of the company
  • Follow up with our employees on a daily basis as part of the mobility plan
  • Manage reporting and data as requested
  • Be involved in various activities in collaboration with your team members and the department
  • Provide input and be part of the vision of the department's activities

WHAT MAKES OUR GROUP DIFFERENT?

Our true power does not lie in our similarities but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures, and human skills, as well as our specific ability to foster untapped potential.

  • We value freedom, collegiality, loyalty, and solidarity.
  • We foster empathy, curiosity, courage, humility, and integrity.
  • We care for the world we live in.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • You are comfortable working in teams but can also manage to work on your own
  • You enjoy interacting with different partners within an organization
  • You are personable and have an excellent team spirit
  • You have a good knowledge of Microsoft Office platforms
  • You have a curious mind and can think out of the box

  • You want to develop administrative skills and bring new ideas
  • You are fluent in French and English, any additional language is a plus

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Genève ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Genève après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Genève ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
