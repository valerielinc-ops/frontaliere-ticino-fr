The opportunity

Hitachi Energy is a pioneer and recognized market leader in Power Conversion and FACTS (Flexible AC Transmission Systems). Our Power Converter Solutions team develops and delivers power-electronics-based systems including Static Frequency Converters (SFC), Static Compensators (STATCOM), and other advanced converter technologies in the power range up to 150 MVA and above. These solutions support utilities and industries worldwide, serving railway power supplies, hydropower applications, grid interties, and renewable integration.

We are looking for an experienced Team Lead System Engineering to lead a team of highly skilled System Engineers while also providing technical leadership for the successful delivery of complex converter projects. In this role, you will combine people leadership, engineering excellence, and strategic execution to ensure our team continues to deliver innovative and high-quality solutions to customers worldwide. The position is part of the Power Converter Solutions engineering organization and plays a key role in shaping the future growth and competence of the team.

How you'll make an impact

  • Lead, coach, and develop a team of System Engineers, fostering a high-performance, collaborative, and accountable engineering culture
  • Provide technical leadership for the design and implementation of power converter systems, including SFCs, STATCOMs, and related solutions, from the sales phase through project execution and the entire project lifecycle
  • Drive successful System Engineering delivery by prioritizing resources, balancing workloads, and removing obstacles
  • Lead and perform key system engineering activities including network studies, harmonic and transient simulations, performance calculations, control and protection concepts, and technical specifications for suppliers
  • Foster close collaboration with Sales, Project Management, R&D, Quality, Supply Chain, Service, and other engineering disciplines to achieve project and business objectives
  • Identify and mitigate technical and organizational risks, while driving continuous improvement, advancements of engineering tools and knowledge sharing
  • Drive continuous improvement of engineering processes and tools while promoting knowledge sharing across the organization
  • Contribute to the successful delivery of converter projects by ensuring strong technical leadership, proactive stakeholder management, and a consistent commitment to safety, quality, schedule, and cost targets

Your background

  • Master's degree or PhD in Electrical Engineering or a related field, ideally with a focus on Power Systems or Power Electronics
  • Strong industrial experience (5+ years) in system engineering, power electronics, grid integration, FACTS, HVDC, converter systems, or related fields
  • Strong technical expertise in power systems, power electronics, and simulation tools used for system design and performance studies
  • Proven leadership experience or demonstrated potential to lead, coach and develop others
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, decision-making, and stakeholder management skills
  • Ability to balance people leadership with technical oversight in a dynamic project-driven environment
  • Experience in railway power supplies, hydropower, FACTS or utility-scale power conversion is an advantage
  • Fluent English
  • German is an advantage
  • Willingness to travel internationally on an occasional basis (<10%)
