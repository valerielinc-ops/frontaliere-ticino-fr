At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. The Position This role is initially for a fixed-term contract 12 months. The employment contract will be concluded by our vendor/third party for outsourcing services. We have a fantastic and challenging opportunity to fully combine your recruiting and consulting skills to seek, attract, and bring in the very best talent into Roche. This is with a focus on supporting the demand for talent within our Roche EMEA region. As a Talent Acquisition Partner you will play a pivotal role in ensuring we are hiring the best talent to meet our business ambitions. Make Your Mark. Improve Lives. You will be part of the Roche EMEA Recruiting team, a group of highly skilled professionals in our Integrated Workforce Strategies organization. The geographic focus will be Extended Eastern Europe (EEE) & CIS countries, however you will also be collaborating closely within the Region EMEA where we swarm our capacities to achieve our region’s goals as one team, and also within our global network. This position reports to the Recruiting Lead EEE & CIS countries. Your Impact

  • Be a professional and consultative partner for our business leaders regarding all talent acquisition aspects
  • Expertly drive end-to-end recruiting processes and provide customized talent acquisition strategies and solutions, including active candidate sourcing & pipelining, talent marketing, talent selection, candidate experience, and Diversity, Equity & Inclusion
  • Manage the offer process with all necessary stakeholders (create an offer proposal following our Roche compensation and benefits framework and pay equity considerations, obtain approvals, extend offers, and negotiate with the candidate if required)
  • Closely collaborate with the other People and Culture colleagues in-country, particularly the Business Partners to have insight about the coming business priorities, and the People Relations Partners to ensure a high confidentiality of recruitment-related topics
  • May lead recruitment transformation projects across functions/countries Who You Are
  • University degree in Human Resources Management, Business Management, Economics, Marketing, Finance is preferred
  • You are fluent in English but also having additional language skills are an advantage
  • You are preferably working in the Life Science/Tech industry and have a high passion for People and Culture, particularly for Talent Acquisition with proven success as a recruiter or head-hunter
  • You are well-versed in talent attraction, sourcing, and assessing across multiple geographies and varied levels of complexity and expert in applicant tracking and sourcing tools such as Workday, Phenom People, LinkedIn
  • You excel at building and maintaining relationships with stakeholders across all levels, both within our business and the People and Culture team, leveraging excellent communication, interpersonal, and influencing skills to collaborate, negotiate, and innovate effectively
  • You have excellent coaching and advising skills, and thus are a trusted partner to your business clients and enable them in their hiring decisions
  • You can work in a multinational matrix organization with the dexterity to participate in or lead recruiting squads across regions or globally Who we are A healthier future drives us to innovate.
