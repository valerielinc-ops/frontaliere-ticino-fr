The Position As the Recruiting Lead, Operations & Quality (US), this is your moment to shape how Roche & Genentech brings the deep and exceptional talent into our US operations & quality functions, powering our next chapter of growth. We are at the threshold of exciting strategic expansions in the US, and we need a recruiting leader who thrives in delivering fit-for-purpose talent acquisition strategies that give our business a competitive edge. You will lead a high-impact team of Talent Acquisition Partners (TAPs) in a fluid, networked environment, balancing global consistency with the local agility needed to win in this competitive and critical market. This is a role for a transformational leader, relentlessly inspiring high performance in your team, and continuously raising the bar of excellence.

The Opportunity

  • Mentor and coach a dedicated community of TAPs, elevating the recruiting team to a high-performing advisory group that consistently delivers value through strategic partnership and talent advisory.
  • Continuously strive to enable the team by removing barriers, building high-value capabilities, influencing change adoption, accelerating learning, and empowering high performance.
  • Move beyond reactive hiring by partnering with functional and geographic senior leaders to ensure proactive recruitment strategies that align directly with our business priorities.
  • Turn market insights and hiring data into a competitive advantage, utilizing analytics to drive smarter sourcing and innovative solutions for complex talent gaps across the US Operations & Quality network.
  • Ensure our global recruiting standards are upheld while having the autonomy to localize strategies that resonate within the US market.
