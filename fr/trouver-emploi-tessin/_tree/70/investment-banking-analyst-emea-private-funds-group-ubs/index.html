Investment Banking Analyst - EMEA Private Funds Group — UBS

Ce poste n'est plus actif. Vous trouverez ci-dessous les détails originaux et d’autres postes ouverts.

Description complète

You will join the UBS Private Funds Group (PFG) within Global Banking, with a primary focus on primary fundraising advisory. PFG provides global advisory and distribution services to private fund sponsors across private equity, private debt, infrastructure and real assets, combining primary fundraising capabilities with secondary and liquidity solutions. In this role, you will support the origination and execution of fundraising mandates, working closely with senior PFG bankers, global distribution colleagues and clients. You will play a key role in evaluating opportunities, preparing marketing materials, coordinating investor outreach and supporting successful capital raises. The role also offers exposure to GP‑led and LP‑led secondary transactions. You will be responsible for a broad range of execution, advisory and project management activities, including:

  • working with senior PFG team members to evaluate prospective fundraising opportunities, including detailed analysis of client portfolios, track records, teams, strategies and market opportunities
  • advising clients on fundraising strategy, including fund structuring, timing, investor targeting and legal and commercial terms
  • articulating complex investment and marketing messages clearly and effectively to institutional investors
  • leading the preparation of key fundraising materials, including private placement memoranda, investor presentations, due diligence questionnaires and financial models
  • planning, building and managing virtual data rooms to support investor due diligence
  • maintaining comprehensive fundraising trackers, coordinating investor outreach, managing feedback and producing regular internal and external reports
  • supporting the organisation and execution of investor roadshows, liaising with global distribution colleagues and roadshow coordinators
