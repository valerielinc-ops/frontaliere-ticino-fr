The Material Handler performs the physical or administrative tasks involved in shipping, receiving, order fulfillment, storing and distributing of materials, parts, supplies and equipment, and supporting logistics areas. This may include receiving and verifying in-coming materials and supplies, preparing bills of lading and shipping manifests, routing shipments appropriately, moving material within the warehouse, distribution and production facilities, filling customers’ orders and preparing orders appropriately. Acquires and applies job skills, company policies and procedures to complete assigned tasks. Assignments are routine to semi-routine in nature. Works under supervision and receives detailed instructions on work assignments. The opportunity:

  • Pick items either by RF or voice picking. Pack, ship, receive and maintain inventory of product/raw material, and fulfill customer orders with emphasis on attention to detail.
  • Examine, stock, and distribute materials in inventory.
  • Accurately pull and/or pack products for customer orders ensuring lot numbers, shipping temperature, address and ship methods are correct. May pick and pack in different temperature environments (ex: refrigerated, frozen, ambient)
  • Receive all incoming materials stored at the correct temperature. Inspect and compare with packing lists, bills of lading, purchase orders and/or related documentation. Report discrepancies or damage goods. Document and label materials according to procedures.
  • Perform administrative tasks related to products/materials such as coordinating shipments, preparing, reviewing, filling and maintaining customer orders, preparing shipping and receiving documents, and utilizing database to enter and maintain information related to products/material.
  • Identify issues involving logistics, customer orders, shipping and/or receiving areas.
  • May operate material handling equipment as necessary including a forklift and man-up order picker.
  • Follow the principles and applications of Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and ISO 13485.
  • Responsible for maintaining and continuously improving the quality system and achieving quality objectives through daily actions. This is a fixed duration position for up to 3 years. Who you are? Minimum qualifications:
