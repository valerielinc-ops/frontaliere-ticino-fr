The Position Introduction

At Roche, we are committed to delivering greater benefits to our patients. To achieve that, the Performance Excellence Lead is a hands-on Operational Excellence expert to drive breakthrough performance improvements and build long-term capabilities. This role is about delivering step-change improvements, not just marginal gains, by embedding structured, data-driven methodologies that enhance efficiency, quality, and overall operational performance. As a member of the Performance Excellence Delivery team, you work with site teams and functions, integrating traditional Lean principles with cutting-edge digital solutions to drive true transformation using our five-step Breakthrough Methodology: Preparation, Diagnose, Design, Implement, and Sustain.

The Opportunity

  • Lead structured breakthrough initiatives that deliver step-change improvements in operational quality, efficiency, and productivity rather than incremental gains.
  • Own the end-to-end delivery of Performance Excellence initiatives across all core phases: Project Scoping, Preparation, Diagnose, Design, Build & Deploy, and Sustain.
  • Structure and solve complex operational challenges by guiding cross-functional teams toward measurable, high-impact business solutions.
  • Establish and track key initiative performance indicators to ensure long-term, tangible business value and operational continuity.
  • Apply operational pillars—including Operational Excellence (OE), Lean, Digital, AI, and Data—to eliminate waste and optimize end-to-end processes.
  • Deploy systematic, proven, data-driven frameworks to drive hands-on execution and ensure repeatable performance gains across site operations.
  • Coach and upskill project teams in Lean and OE tools, fostering an inclusive, high-performance culture that anchors sustainable capability building.
  • Partner with site functions and senior leadership to secure organizational buy-in, manage change, and align strategic operational enhancements.

Who you are

  • Education: Hold a Bachelor’s degree (required) or an Advanced degree (Master’s/PhD preferred) in a relevant technical, engineering, or business field.
  • Years of Experience: Bring a minimum of 8 years of relevant operational excellence experience with a Bachelor’s degree, or 6+ years of equivalent experience with an Advanced degree.
  • Industry Background: Possess hands-on professional experience within biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, chemical manufacturing, or related process industries (e.g., manufacturing, supply chain, or quality).
  • Operational Excellence Mastery: Demonstrate proven expertise in leading structured OE and Lean methodologies to drive step-change improvements.
  • Digital & Data Savvy: Skilled in synthesizing complex data sets into actionable insights and supporting the adoption of OE, Digital, AI, and advanced data solutions.
  • Leadership & Influence: Exhibit strong stakeholder management skills with the confidence to influence, challenge senior leadership, and maintain project accountability.
  • Growth Mindset & Communication: Act as an agile, collaborative thought partner with excellent written and verbal communication skills across cross-functional teams.
