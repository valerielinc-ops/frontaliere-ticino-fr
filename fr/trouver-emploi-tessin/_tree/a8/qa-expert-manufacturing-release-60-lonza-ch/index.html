Join our Drug Product Services team in Stein, Switzerland – a leading CDMO branch shaping the future of patient treatment. Be part of a team that drives excellence and innovation in global healthcare! This is an ideal opportunity for people who wish to work parttime 60%. This is a fully site‑based role.
