Credit Analyst / Junior Portfolio Manager • Basel, CH; Zurich, CH • Full-time • Unlimited Your Role As Credit Analyst / Junior Portfolio Manager at Bank J. Safra Sarasin you are a curious and ambitious individual with 2–4 years of experience in credit analysis of bank and financials, seeking to contribute to the success of our fixed income team and to develop toward portfolio management over time. You work closely with the senior portfolio managers and you provide investment recommendation for banks across the full capital stack. You support in portfolio management tasks and help in creating marketing materials, including reports, articles and pitch books. As your views mature and your ownership grows, portfolio management is a natural career path for you.

Your Responsibilities

  • Provide investment recommendations for banks and financials, monitoring the market and portfolio holdings to identify investment and trade opportunities
  • Contribute to investment discussions, sharing your insights and views across the broader team
  • Support the portfolio managers in daily management tasks including trade preparation, cash flow management and portfolio analytics
  • Act as portfolio deputy to build your portfolio management skills
  • Prepare performance reports, marketing materials and investment content for internal and external circulation

Your Profile

  • Higher education (university / FH) in finance, business or a related field; CFA or CAIA certifications are a plus
  • 2 - 4 years of practical experience in bank / financials credit analysis, ideally in a practical investment orientated role; coverage of European banks is a strong plus
  • Excellent Microsoft Office skills and experience with Bloomberg
  • Strong analytical and problem solving skills, with the ability to weigh problems and set priorities
  • Ability to work with various stakeholders; excellent interpersonal, communication and presentation skills
  • Capability to work independently, with a hands-on mentality and willingness to take responsibility
  • Fluency in English; fluency in German is a plus
