Description complète

Fincons Group is an IT business consulting company that has been designing the digital future of leading companies on international markets for 40 years. Fincons Group built its reputation on foreseeing and interpreting new business models and the rapid evolution of IT systems by building a complete range of services: from research and consulting to design and development, from system integration of leading vendor software solutions right up to application management, supporting clients step by step along their digital transformation journey. Fincons is a multinational with over 2,600 people and several offices worldwide (in Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Belgium and the USA), but above all, a Family Company where the founders play a strategic role with commitment and passion, grounding the company in the same principles of a united and caring family. We firmly believe in the value of cooperation and in the contribution that every idea and intuition can bring. We believe that everyone’s effort can make a difference. Transforming knowledge into a strategic resource is our company mission, and we pursue it with the help of our most strategic asset: our people. Job Overview We are seeking a proactive, quality-focused Mobile Developer with a solid understanding of the mobile development lifecycle and modern app architecture. In this role, you will contribute to the design and development of advanced mobile solutions, leveraging modern technologies such as Kotlin Multiplatform and Kotlin Native , while ensuring high standards of quality, performance, and reliability. You will be responsible for writing clean, scalable, and maintainable code and collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver robust mobile applications. Key Responsibilities

  • Designing and building scalable, reusable mobile solutions
  • Developing and maintaining mobile features using Kotlin Multiplatform and Kotlin Native
  • Writing clean, high-performing, and maintainable code
  • Testing and optimizing features for performance, usability, and reliability
  • Troubleshooting and resolving bugs, edge cases, and performance issues
  • Collaborating with cross-functional teams to define and implement new features
  • Contributing to product design and feature planning with the product team
  • Documenting features and technical implementations clearly
  • Championing clean architecture, security, and performance best practices

Required Qualifications and Skills

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent experience
  • Strong experience developing Android and iOS applications
  • Broad experience in software design and software development using Java and Kotlin
  • Practical knowledge of Kotlin Multiplatform and Kotlin Native
  • Strong experience in Android Native / Android SDK mobile development
  • Excellent understanding of software architecture, design patterns, test-driven development, and source code management
  • Experience with MVVM, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines, Flow, and REST API integrations
  • A strong grasp of mobile security, performance, and responsive UI design
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills
  • Ability to communicate effectively with team members and stakeholders in English and Italian
  • Experience working in Agile teams and mentoring others is a plus

Location Requirement

  • Please note that a fully remote work policy is not offered for this role
  • Candidates should be prepared to relocate to Canton Ticino/Switzerland or towns near the Swiss-Italian border

Oui. Pour les citoyens UE/AELE, le permis G ne dépend plus d'une zone frontalière : il faut résider dans un État UE/AELE, avoir un contrat suisse et rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. Le permis vaut cinq ans avec un contrat à durée indéterminée ou de plus d'un an, sinon pour la durée du contrat.
