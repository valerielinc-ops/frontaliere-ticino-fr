The Position *12 Month Opportunity* Roche is a global healthcare leader at the forefront of innovation, dedicated to improving the lives of patients through the development of groundbreaking medicines and diagnostic tools. With a heritage spanning over 125 years, we are committed to sustainable healthcare and delivering medical solutions that address some of the world’s most complex health challenges. Our Global Clinical Supply Chain Organization is the backbone of our clinical research, ensuring that no patient goes without treatment and no clinical trial is delayed. By joining our newly expanded team in Mississauga, Canada, you will be part of a strategically significant hub that fosters agility, cross-functional collaboration, and the delivery of life-saving biopharmaceuticals to more than 100 molecules across 600+ clinical trials globally. The Opportunity

  • You will take ownership of the clinical study supply chain for Investigational Medicinal Products (IMP), ensuring effective and cost-conscious delivery across diverse disease areas.
  • You will execute end-to-end demand and supply planning within ERP systems, such as SAP, to maintain robust aggregate and long-range program oversight.
  • You will lead the alignment of key activities and timelines across cross-functional teams to ensure every clinical trial is supplied in strict accordance with agreed scopes.
  • You will strategically forecast, plan, and initiate the procurement of comparator drugs and other study materials required for complex clinical protocols.
  • You will integrate all relevant regulatory and quality requirements into the supply plan to ensure compliance with international standards and GMP environments.
  • You will drive business excellence by owning and optimizing Clinical Supply Chain processes, influencing change, and ensuring global system integration.
  • You will collaborate with Technical Development Teams and Clinical Operations to bridge the gap between drug development and patient-centric delivery. Who you are
  • You possess a minimum of five years of experience in Clinical Supply Chain, Planning, or Manufacturing, ideally within the Biotechnology or Pharmaceutical industry.
  • You hold an academic degree in Life Sciences, Business, or Engineering, complemented by a solid understanding of clinical trial design and drug technical development.
  • You are skilled at navigating ERP systems and IRT functionalities to manage complex inventory and supply chain optimization simulations.
  • You demonstrate a high level of proficiency in GMP environments, with a keen eye for quality assurance, regulatory affairs, and budget planning.
  • You are a confident stakeholder manager who excels at building customer focus and earning respect through impactful, needs-driven actions.
  • You possess an agile mindset, showing a willingness to experiment with new ideas and lead process improvement activities in a self-directed team environment.
