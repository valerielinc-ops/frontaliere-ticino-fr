Aufgaben

  • Portfolio & Strategy: Responsible for the complete coffee processing solution portfolio (research, automation, training, engineering, marketing). Drive the implementation of the Flavor Creation strategy to strengthen market leadership.

  • University Degree: In Engineering or Food Technology.

  • Experience: Minimum 5 years of professional experience in international sales of capital goods.
  • Industry Background: Preferably in technology-based industries, processing, or plant engineering (specifically coffee or food processing is a strong plus).

  • Leadership: Ability to lead a team as well as work collaboratively as a team member. Strong assertiveness and powers of persuasion.
  • Strategic Thinking: Ability to formulate technology roadmaps and assess business ideas.
  • Conflict Management: Ability to manage conflicts effectively..
  • Personality: Business-minded, proactive, creative, and highly customer-driven with a competent appearance.
  • Work Style: High level of autonomy, independence, and above-average personal commitment.
  • Cultural Fit: Receptiveness to other cultures and preparedness for international travel. Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment.
