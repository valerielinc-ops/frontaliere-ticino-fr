The Opportunity

We are looking for a Scientific Data Scientist & Engineer – DNA-Encoded Library Technology (DELT) to join our team at the intersection of chemical biology, cheminformatics, and data science.

In this role, you will help shape the next generation of data-driven DNA-Encoded Library Technology (DELT) by combining scientific data analysis, scalable data engineering, and advanced computational approaches. You will transform complex screening data into actionable scientific insights and develop robust, automated workflows that accelerate hit identification and support small-molecule drug discovery.

Working closely with experimental scientists, computational chemists, and machine learning experts, you will bridge scientific innovation and practical implementation, enabling more informed, reproducible, and efficient decision-making across our DELT platform.

Your impact

  • You advance our DELT data analytics by applying robust statistical methods, predictive models, and advanced analytical approaches to interpret large-scale DEL screening datasets, identify promising chemical starting points, and support hit and lead discovery programs.
  • You develop intuitive, accessible analytical tools and workflows that empower DELT screening scientists and project teams to rapidly evaluate selection results, prioritise compounds, and make informed experimental decisions.
  • You design, implement, and maintain automated, reproducible data processing and analysis pipelines that transform complex experimental outputs into reliable, decision-ready insights.
  • You develop computational workflows and searchable building-block data resources to support chemical space exploration, reaction prediction, library design, and synthesis strategy optimisation.
  • You partner closely with experimental scientists, computational chemists, and machine learning teams across Roche's small-molecule research organisation to translate evolving scientific challenges into scalable computational solutions.
  • You communicate scientific insights by presenting analytical results, computational approaches, and technical developments clearly to scientific and multidisciplinary audiences, supporting knowledge sharing and adoption of new capabilities.
  • You champion scientific excellence by promoting reproducibility, data integrity, and robust engineering practices while contributing to a safe and compliant research environment.

Who you are

You are highly passionate about DNA-encoded library technology, pushing the boundaries of the field, as well as discovering and taking on new challenges. We are looking for a highly motivated data scientist with a strong enthusiasm for chemical biology and pushing the technology to the next step, who is interested in performing state-of-the-art research in the pharmaceutical industry.

  • You hold a PhD or M.S. in Bioinformatics, Statistics, Computer Science, Data Science, Cheminformatics, Software Engineering, or a related quantitative field.
  • You have a strong software engineering background with experience deploying pipelines at scale in HPC or cloud platforms.
  • You possess proven experience in high-throughput scientific data analysis (preferably with large-scale screening data, DEL selection outputs, or NGS processing pipelines) and a demonstrated ability to architect reliable, user-friendly tools that simplify complex analytical workflows for non-expert scientists.
  • You demonstrate strong proficiency in Python and statistical programming, along with expertise in data visualisation frameworks (e.g., Spotfire, Tableau, or modern web dashboarding tools).
  • You bring an understanding of hit-triaging, noise reduction, statistical ranking, and signal verification in large-scale datasets, with demonstrated capability in cheminformatics (including handling chemical structures, building-block metadata, or toolkits like RDKit/OpenEye) and laboratory data systems (LIMS, SQL, relational databases), integrating computational tools with experimental workflows as a plus.
  • You are a critical thinker and problem solver with a continuous improvement mindset and a track record of conceptualising scalable scientific solutions, paired with strong communication skills to translate complex quantitative findings for multidisciplinary and non-quantitative stakeholders.
