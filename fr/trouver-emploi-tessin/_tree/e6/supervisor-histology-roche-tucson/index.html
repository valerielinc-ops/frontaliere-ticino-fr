The Position As the supervisor of Histology, you will lead the Operations team that generates control slides for internal and external customers. This team sections all tissue used for titering of raw materials, final release testing for instruments and reagent quality control, and all finished goods produced containing tissue. You will be responsible for obtaining procured tissue and ensuring all work is compliant with quality policies and procedures as they apply to material traceability, grossing, processing, embedding, microtomy, and kitting in operations. You will oversee both full-time and contingent Histology professionals ranging from entry-level staff with minimal histology experience to senior Histotechnicians and cell culture technicians across multiple shifts. Your main responsibilities include ensuring employee safety, maintaining quality standards for finished goods, and guaranteeing that all departmental output is completed on time and in full according to organizational demand. Additionally, you will be regarded as the expert in tissue-related processes, including tissue identification and use in design transfer, troubleshooting quality control issues, and providing feedback on tissue quality and availability in operations.
