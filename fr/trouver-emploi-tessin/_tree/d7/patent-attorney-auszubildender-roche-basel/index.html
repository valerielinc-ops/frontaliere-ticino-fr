At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position We are seeking a Patent Attorney Trainee with a biochemistry / biomedical background. As a member of our group responsible for Large Molecule pharmaceutical products (biologicals) you will advise our scientists on the patenting of inventions in this field. On your path to develop as European Patent Attorney, you will secure patent protection, freedom to operate and market exclusivity during the life cycle of Roche´s products in close collaboration with the members of all functions involved.

The opportunity

  • Handling of invention reports and drafting of patent applications in close collaboration with Research & Development
  • Handling of patent aspects for Large Molecule projects and products, including prosecution, defense and enforcement of patent rights
  • Patent portfolio management, supporting the decision making process for worldwide filings, maintenance and abandonment of patent filings
  • Performing freedom to operate analysis
  • Review of planned publications
  • Evaluation of contract terms related to IP and coordination with the Legal and Licensing departments on the drafting and negotiating of same
  • Preparation for the European Qualification Examination under supervision of an experienced patent attorney

Who you are

  • Biochemistry / biomedical (or related) background, at least with a master degree, and a strong and broadly based interest in science and technology
  • Strong interest in patent law and interdisciplinary matters with a will to broaden horizons in this field and taking pleasure in a variety of intellectually stimulating work
  • Ideally, 1 to 2 years experience as a Trainee Patent Attorney in a Patent Law firm or equivalent
  • English language skills; knowledge of additional languages, in particular German, will be an advantage
  • Well-developed analytical and writing skills
  • Finely honed interpersonal skills and collaborative mindset
  • Ability to work to tight deadlines and to handle several projects simultaneously
  • Well-structured, reliable and precise style of work
  • Strong business acumen and interest in the pharmaceutical industry

Who we are A healthier future drives us to innovate. Together, more than 100’000 employees across the globe are dedicated to advance science, ensuring everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Our efforts result in more than 26 million people treated with our medicines and over 30 billion tests conducted using our Diagnostics products. We empower each other to explore new possibilities, foster creativity, and keep our ambitions high, so we can deliver life-changing healthcare solutions that make a global impact.

Let’s build a healthier future, together.
