The Position Are you a Finance or Business student looking to gain hands-on experience in a global corporate environment? We are looking for a motivated Intern to join our Global Procurement Finance team. This is a unique opportunity to see the "inner workings" of a global finance function. You will gain exposure to complex stakeholder management and international reporting standards-all while being part of a supportive, expert team. The opportunity In this role, you won’t just be crunching numbers; you’ll be a key part of a team operating across a global scale. You will work directly under the Finance Functional Partner to support global operations and strategic procurement. As our Intern you will:

  • Drive Financial Insights: Assist in preparing monthly financial reports
  • Master the Tools: Take ownership of our procurement savings tracking tool (SpendHQ) - validating business cases and savings entered by the procurement organization.
  • Business Partnering: Collaborate directly with one function/team to provide financial support.
  • Support & Innovate: Help Procurement Finance Business Partners with insight generation, SMC planning, and organizational training. Who you are You are enrolled in or have completed your studies within the last 12 months with a degree (Bachelor’s, Master’s) in Business Administration, Finance, or a related field.

  • Communication: Fluent in English; you’re comfortable working in a diverse, international environment.
