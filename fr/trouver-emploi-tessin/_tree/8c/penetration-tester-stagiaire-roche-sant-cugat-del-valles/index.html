The Position Department Summary We are seeking a highly motivated professional with experience in Security and Privacy to join our dynamic team. As a Junior Penetration Tester, your main responsibilities will include supporting and assisting in penetration testing activities to ensure the security of Roche products and solutions. This role involves collaborating with Product Teams, executing predefined test strategies, and documenting the results. You will play a crucial role in identifying vulnerabilities and aiding in the reporting process. The Opportunity

  • Work closely with Product Teams to understand and determine testing requirements and assist in the development, planning, scoping and execution of testing activities (both in physical and cloud products).
  • Conduct cybersecurity penetration testing activities across various technology layers, assessing the criticality of vulnerabilities for adequate prioritization, and providing suitable remediation recommendations to product teams.
  • Maintain and utilize the repository of penetration testing methods and tools to ensure consistency and standardization in testing practices.
  • Create detailed reports and present findings, associated risks, and recommendations to product teams and management.
  • Participate in planning and managing red team exercises to assess the cyber resilience of products and solutions.
  • Support the Incident Response team during incidents.
  • Contribute to initiatives aimed at integrating defense capabilities into products in collaboration with other PSPO colleagues.
  • Develop, maintain, and continuously optimize processes, playbooks, and tools to enhance penetration testing efficiency and effectiveness. Program Highlights

  • Knowledge of automating security controls through scripting languages such as shell scripting and Python.
  • Understanding of information security and privacy risks.
  • Problem solving abilities, communication, teamwork, flexibility and adaptability.
  • Team player, proactive, self-driven, solution-oriented, and hands-on. Required Majors: Information Systems, Computer Science or a related relevant area of study.
