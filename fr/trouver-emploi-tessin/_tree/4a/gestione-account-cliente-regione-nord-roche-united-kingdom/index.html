The Position The Opportunity As a Customer Account Manager for our Managed Service Contracts in the North region, you will act as the vital link between Roche and our healthcare partners. You will ensure our customers receive a superior experience while bringing their voice directly into our organization to shape the future of diagnostics.

  • Build meaningful partnerships: Develop and maintain long-term commercial relationships across all levels of your assigned customer base.
  • Drive strategic value: Connect customer business goals with Roche’s product and service suite to support their success during periods of healthcare transformation.
  • Lead service delivery: Co-create Strategic account plans and coordinate cross-functional teams (Sales, Marketing, Technical Services) to ensure seamless execution.
  • Manage the lifecycle: Oversee the full customer experience for our Optimall branded Managed Services, serving as the primary point of escalation for Roche and third-party partners.
  • Foster a culture of excellence: Champion the "voice of the customer" internally to ensure we continue to "do now what patients need next." Who You Are You are a strategic thinker and a passionate customer advocate who thrives on delivering value and building resilient commercial relationships.
  • Experienced Account Manager: You bring a proven track record in contract management and commercial engagement within the healthcare sector.
  • Strategic & Analytical: You possess the ability to interrogate large datasets to draw meaningful insights (Advanced Excel) and understand complex business workflows.
  • Collaborative Leader: You are skilled at managing multiple complex projects simultaneously while working alongside experts in technical support, finance, and marketing.
  • Effective Communicator: You are a strong listener who can identify pain points and develop clear solutions, maintaining a professional demeanor under pressure.
  • Education: You are educated to degree level or equivalent in a relevant field. Together, we’ll create a healthier, brighter future for all. Who we are A healthier future drives us to innovate.
