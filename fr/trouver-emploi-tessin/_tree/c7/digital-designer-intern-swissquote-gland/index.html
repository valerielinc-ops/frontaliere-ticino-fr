Description complète

  • UI/UX Design & Prototyping: Create intuitive and engaging digital experiences using Figma, from initial wireframes to high-fidelity designs and interactive prototypes.
  • Digital & Visual Design: Design creative assets for the Swissquote website, email campaigns, social media, and other digital communication channels.
  • AI-Powered Creativity: Explore and experiment with generative AI tools (such as Adobe Firefly, ChatGPT, Midjourney, or similar) to support brainstorming, visual creation, image editing, and creative workflows.
  • AI Skills & Innovation: Develop effective prompting techniques, discover how AI can enhance design processes, and stay curious about emerging AI trends and technologies.
  • Web Integration & Content Management: Get hands-on experience with our CMS to create, update, and publish web pages while learning the fundamentals of digital content integration.
  • Team Collaboration: Support the Design team with day-to-day creative tasks, contribute ideas, and collaborate on various digital projects.
  • 3D Design (optional): Discover tools such as Spline and explore how 3D elements and interactive visuals can enrich digital experiences.
  • Currently studying graphic design, digital design, UI/UX, multimedia, or a related field.
  • Familiar with Figma, Jitter (Motion) and Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects).
  • Interested in generative AI and emerging creative technologies. Previous experience with AI tools is a plus, but curiosity, motivation, and a willingness to learn are what matter most.
  • A good eye for design, attention to detail, and enthusiasm for creating engaging digital experiences.
  • Eager to learn about web integration, CMS platforms, and digital content management.
  • A collaborative, proactive, and open-minded attitude, with good communication skills.
  • Independence & Precision: Well-organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable taking initiative while being open to feedback.
  • Native-level French and a good level of English is recommended.
  • Basic knowledge of 3D design tools such as Spline and an interest in the banking and finance industry.
