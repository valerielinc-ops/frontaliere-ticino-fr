Head of AI Product — Artificialy

Ce poste n'est plus actif. Vous trouverez ci-dessous les détails originaux et d’autres postes ouverts.

Description complète

Artificialy is an AI company based in Switzerland, with offices in Lugano and Zurich. We design and deliver AI solutions that are impactful, transparent, and tailored to real-world industry needs.

Founded in 2020 by pioneers with over 40 years of combined experience in the field, our team brings together talented engineers, physicists, and mathematicians from across Europe. We are driven by curiosity, technical excellence, and a shared ambition to build AI systems that make a tangible difference.

We are looking for a Head of AI Product who leads vision, strategy and go-to-market of new company’s products, with a strong focus on Artificialy AI technologies.

This role aims to leverage results, artefacts, prototypes, and ideas developed by Artificialy to translate them into market-ready AI products, with a strong focus on business strategy, market positioning and growth.

About the Role

  • You will work on the front line with a team (initially small) which collaborates with various departments, including engineering and sales to develop and launch new high-quality products
  • You will translate technical capabilities into scalable, market-ready AI products and business value, with a strong focus on go-to-market execution and competitive positioning
  • You will own the overall product portfolio and lifecycle, including business planning, commercial strategy, and stakeholder management

Core Responsibilities and tasks

  • Product & Business Strategy: Defines strategy, vision and roadmap for AI-enabled products, balancing market size, business value, competitive landscape, technical constraints and model maturity
  • Go-to-Market: Design and execute go-to-market plans (positioning, pricing, target segments, channel and partnership strategy) for new AI products. Define the best go-to-market approach for target clients (direct or via partners), understanding client needs and buying dynamics
  • Competitive & Market Analysis: Continuously monitor the competitive landscape and the fast-evolving AI product market to identify opportunities, threats and differentiation strategies
  • Product Development: Manage the entire product lifecycle, from discovery to industrialization, accelerating development and time to market
  • AI Production: Collaborate with a cross-functional product teams ensuring agility and alignment and a smooth transition from prototype to production
  • Validation and Lean: Apply Lean Startup methodologies to validate business hypotheses, test MVPs, gather user feedback, and monitor KPIs
  • Stakeholder Management: Liaise with internal and external stakeholders, partners and investors to present progress and results

Qualifications and Skills

  • Proven experience (about 5-10 years) in Product Acceleration in the ICT sector, preferably in AI
  • Bachelor/Master/PhD Degree in economics, engineering, or a related field
  • Strong track record in go-to-market strategy, competitive analysis and commercial product launches
  • Previous success launching and scaling digital products, with a clear focus on business outcomes
  • Solid understanding of AI technologies and the ability to translate technical capabilities into business value
  • Strong leadership and cross-functional collaboration abilities
  • Deep understanding of product development, product analytics and market research
  • Fluency in English (min. C1)

Nice to have

  • MBA or an advanced degree
  • Experience in startups or scale-ups, especially in digital products, AI, or technology-driven company
  • Knowledge about Italian/German and in general Swiss Languages is a plus

We offer

  • Full-time permanent contract
  • Possibility to work from home
  • Competitive compensation and growth opportunities
  • A stimulating scientific environment with an informal working atmosphere
