This role is ideal for someone who is curious about technology, enjoys simplifying complex ideas, and is eager to learn how automation and data-driven solutions can create business impact. You will be part of RSS Business Analytics - a global team with regional presence in Switzerland, India, Costa Rica, Hungary, Latvia and Malaysia. We operate in an agile organizational set-up without extensive hierarchical structure, where all our employees lead from every seat. Inspirational leadership, service oriented behavior and entrepreneurial customer centric mindset are our core values. As part of your 6-month journey, you will have the chance to dive into one or more of these core areas:
